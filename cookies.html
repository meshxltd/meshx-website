1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device. We use the word “cookies” here to include similar technologies such as local storage where relevant.

2. How we use cookies today

On meshx.uk we currently aim to use only cookies and similar storage that are strictly necessary for the site to function (for example security or basic preferences). We do not use advertising or analytics cookies on this marketing site unless we notify you and update this policy.

When you use our application products (MeshX Create, MeshX Shop Manager, MeshX Print Network), additional cookies or tokens may be required for sign-in and session security. Those uses are described in the Privacy policy.

3. Third-party content

If we embed content or tools from third parties in future, their cookies may apply. We will list common examples here when we add them.

4. Your choices

You can block or delete cookies through your browser settings. Blocking strictly necessary cookies may prevent parts of the site or app from working.
